# KhaanaShare Food Listing Policy

**INTERNAL POLICY - KhaanaShare Platform**

## Provider Safety Confirmation

All food listings on KhaanaShare require an explicit provider safety confirmation before publication. This confirmation represents the provider's attestation that:

1. The food has been prepared, handled, and stored according to applicable food safety guidelines
2. The food is safe for consumption at the time of listing
3. All preparation and storage information provided is accurate
4. The provider has not knowingly listed unsafe or spoiled food

## Required Listing Information

Every food listing must include:

- **Food title and description**: Accurate description of the food item
- **Preparation time**: When the food was prepared
- **Storage information**: How the food has been stored since preparation
- **Ingredients**: Complete list of ingredients for allergen disclosure
- **Allergens**: Declaration of known allergens (e.g., nuts, dairy, gluten)
- **Dietary information**: Relevant dietary restrictions (e.g., vegetarian, halal)
- **Pickup window**: Time frame when food must be collected
- **Quantity and servings**: Accurate count of available servings

## Prohibited Claims

Providers must not:

- Claim food is "certified safe" by KhaanaShare or any platform
- Make unsubstantiated health claims about the food
- List food known to be spoiled, contaminated, or unsafe
- Misrepresent preparation or storage conditions
- List food prepared by unlicensed commercial entities (unless verified)

## Listing Safety Boundaries

KhaanaShare does not:

- Certify food safety
- Inspect food preparation facilities
- Conduct food safety testing
- Provide food safety certifications
- Guarantee that listed food is safe to consume

**Food safety remains the provider's responsibility.** KhaanaShare provides a platform for food rescue but does not replace professional food safety oversight.

## AI Assistant Limitations

The Mano AI assistant can:

- Provide general food safety guidance from approved sources
- Explain food safety regulations and best practices
- Answer questions about proper food handling
- Reference official food safety documents

The Mano AI assistant cannot:

- Certify specific food items as safe
- Override provider safety confirmations
- Make autonomous food safety decisions
- Provide medical advice
- Diagnose foodborne illness

## Escalation Requirements

If a provider is unsure whether food is safe to list:

1. Do not list the food
2. Consult appropriate food safety guidelines
3. Contact a food safety professional if needed
4. When in doubt, err on the side of caution
