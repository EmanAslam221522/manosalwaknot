# KhaanaShare Incident Escalation Policy

**INTERNAL POLICY - KhaanaShare Platform**

## Incident Types

### Suspected Unsafe Food
Any report of food that may be unsafe to consume, including:
- Reports of foodborne illness after consumption
- Visible signs of spoilage at pickup
- Improper storage or handling by provider
- Known contamination sources

### Spoiled Food
Food that has:
- Off odors, unusual colors, or visible mold
- Been stored improperly
- Exceeded safe consumption timeframes
- Been left at unsafe temperatures

### Policy Violations
Actions that violate platform policies:
- False safety confirmations
- Misrepresentation of food condition
- Repeated no-shows
- Abuse or harassment

## Reporting Process

### Immediate Reporting
Users should report incidents immediately through:
- The platform's reporting system
- Selecting appropriate incident type
- Providing detailed description
- Including photos/evidence when available

### Information Required
- Reservation or food listing ID
- Date and time of incident
- Detailed description of the issue
- Any evidence (photos, messages)
- Contact information for follow-up

## Escalation Triggers

### Automatic Escalation
Incidents automatically escalate to moderators when:
- Health/safety concerns are reported
- Multiple reports involve the same provider
- Pattern of policy violations is detected
- Recipient reports illness after consumption

### Manual Escalation
Moderators may escalate when:
- Evidence suggests serious policy violation
- User health may be at risk
- Legal or regulatory implications exist
- Reputation impact to platform is significant

## Escalation Levels

### Level 1: Community Review
- Initial report received
- Automated flagging for pattern detection
- Provider/recipient notified
- Response time: Within 24 hours

### Level 2: Moderator Review
- Manual review by platform moderator
- Evidence collection and verification
- Decision on appropriate action
- Response time: Within 48 hours

### Level 3: Admin Review
- Serious or repeated violations
- Potential account suspension
- Legal or regulatory involvement
- Response time: Within 72 hours

### Level 4: Emergency Escalation
- Immediate health/safety risk
- Multiple severe reports
- Legal or regulatory emergency
- Immediate action required

## Actions and Consequences

### For Providers
- Warning for first minor violation
- Temporary listing suspension for moderate violations
- Account suspension for serious or repeated violations
- Permanent ban for severe violations or fraud

### For Recipients
- Warning for first minor violation
- Temporary reservation restrictions for moderate violations
- Account suspension for abuse or harassment
- Permanent ban for fraud or malicious reporting

### For Volunteers
- Warning for first minor violation
- Temporary delivery restrictions for moderate violations
- Account suspension for serious violations
- Permanent ban for theft or serious misconduct

## Appeal Process

Users may appeal decisions within 7 days by:
- Submitting appeal through the platform
- Providing additional evidence
- Explaining mitigating circumstances
- Admin review within 14 days

## Confidentiality

Incident reports are:
- Treated as confidential
- Shared only with relevant moderators/admins
- Used for pattern detection and policy improvement
- Not publicly disclosed without consent

## Collaboration with Authorities

KhaanaShare will cooperate with:
- Food safety authorities when required
- Law enforcement for illegal activities
- Health departments for illness outbreaks
- Consumer protection agencies as needed

## Prevention

Platform prevention measures include:
- Provider verification requirements
- Safety confirmation attestations
- Pattern detection and early warning
- User education on food safety
- Clear policy communication
