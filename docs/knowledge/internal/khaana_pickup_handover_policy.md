# KhaanaShare Pickup and Handover Policy

**INTERNAL POLICY - KhaanaShare Platform**

## Pickup Process

### Provider Responsibilities
- Have food ready at the agreed pickup time
- Package food appropriately for transport
- Provide any special handling instructions
- Confirm recipient identity through the platform
- Complete handover process in the app

### Recipient Responsibilities
- Arrive within the specified pickup window
- Bring appropriate containers if needed
- Present confirmation/QR code for verification
- Follow provider's handover instructions
- Confirm receipt in the app

### Volunteer Responsibilities (if applicable)
- Arrive at pickup location on time
- Verify provider identity and food details
- Transport food safely maintaining temperature control
- Deliver to recipient within agreed timeframe
- Complete delivery confirmation in the app

## Handover Verification

### QR Code System
- Handover tokens are time-limited and single-use
- QR codes must be scanned at pickup to verify authenticity
- Tokens expire after the specified time window
- Each reservation has a unique handover token

### Identity Verification
- Providers verify recipient identity through the app
- Volunteers verify both provider and recipient identities
- No food should be handed over without platform verification
- Suspicious activity should be reported

## Temperature Control During Transport

### Hot Foods
- Keep at 60°C (140°F) or above during transport
- Use insulated containers or thermal bags
- Minimize transport time
- Deliver as quickly as possible

### Cold Foods
- Keep at 4°C (40°F) or below during transport
- Use cooler bags with ice packs
- Minimize time outside refrigeration
- Deliver promptly

### General Guidelines
- Transport time should not exceed 1 hour when possible
- Avoid leaving food in vehicles (especially in hot weather)
- Prioritize direct routes for delivery
- If transport time is extended, reassess food safety

## Responsibility Boundaries

### Provider Responsibility Ends
- When food is handed over to verified recipient
- When food is handed over to verified volunteer
- After pickup time window expires with no-show

### Recipient Responsibility Begins
- At the moment of handover
- For all food safety decisions after pickup
- For proper storage and consumption

### Volunteer Responsibility
- During transport only
- For maintaining food safety during delivery
- Not for food quality or safety assessment

## No-Show Handling

### Recipient No-Show
- Provider may reclaim food after pickup window
- Food may be offered to other recipients if still safe
- No-show is recorded in recipient's history
- Repeated no-shows may affect platform access

### Provider No-Show
- Recipient should report through the platform
- Reservation may be cancelled
- Incident is recorded for provider review

## Dispute Resolution

If issues arise during pickup/handover:

1. Document the issue in the app
2. Take photos if appropriate
3. Report through the platform's reporting system
4. Platform moderators will review and mediate
5. Decisions are made based on evidence and policy
