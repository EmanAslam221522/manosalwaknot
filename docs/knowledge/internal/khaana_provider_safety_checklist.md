# KhaanaShare Provider Safety Checklist

**INTERNAL POLICY - KhaanaShare Platform**

## Before Listing Food

Use this checklist to ensure food is safe to list on KhaanaShare:

### Preparation Safety
- [ ] Food was prepared in a clean, sanitary environment
- [ ] Hands were washed before and during food preparation
- [ ] Utensils and surfaces were cleaned and sanitized
- [ ] Food was cooked to appropriate safe temperatures
- [ ] Cross-contamination was prevented (raw and cooked foods separated)

### Storage Safety
- [ ] Food has been stored at safe temperatures (refrigerated if required)
- [ ] Food has been stored in clean, covered containers
- [ ] Storage time limits have been respected
- [ ] Food has not been left at room temperature for extended periods
- [ ] Refrigerator temperature is at or below 4°C (40°F)

### Handling Information
- [ ] Preparation time is accurately recorded
- [ ] Storage conditions are accurately described
- [ ] Any special handling requirements are noted
- [ ] Reheating instructions are provided if applicable

### Allergen Disclosure
- [ ] All ingredients are listed
- [ ] Common allergens are declared (nuts, dairy, eggs, gluten, soy, fish, shellfish)
- [ ] Cross-contamination risks with allergens are noted
- [ ] Dietary information is accurate (vegetarian, vegan, halal, etc.)

### Quality Assessment
- [ ] Food appears and smells normal
- [ ] No signs of spoilage (off odors, unusual colors, mold)
- [ ] Texture is as expected for the food type
- [ ] Food is within its safe consumption window
- [ ] You would feel comfortable serving this food to your family

## Provider Safety Confirmation

By confirming safety on KhaanaShare, you attest that:

1. You have completed this checklist honestly
2. The food meets all safety criteria above
3. You take responsibility for the food's safety
4. You understand KhaanaShare does not certify food safety

## When in Doubt

If you cannot confidently check all items:

- **Do not list the food**
- Consult food safety guidelines
- Dispose of food if there is any uncertainty
- Safety of recipients is the priority
