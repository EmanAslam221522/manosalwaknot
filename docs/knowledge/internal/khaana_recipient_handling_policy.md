# KhaanaShare Recipient Handling Policy

**INTERNAL POLICY - KhaanaShare Platform**

## Recipient Responsibilities

Recipients of food through KhaanaShare are responsible for:

### Food Collection
- Arriving at the pickup location within the specified pickup window
- Bringing appropriate containers if required
- Following any special handling instructions provided by the provider
- Confirming receipt of food through the platform

### Food Handling After Pickup
- Transporting food safely (keeping hot foods hot, cold foods cold)
- Storing food at appropriate temperatures promptly after pickup
- Consuming food within safe time limits
- Following any reheating instructions provided

### Food Safety Assessment
Recipients should:

- Visually inspect food before consumption
- Check for signs of spoilage (off odors, unusual colors, mold)
- Trust their senses - if food seems off, do not consume it
- Reheat food to appropriate temperatures if required
- Consume perishable food within recommended timeframes

### Allergen Awareness
- Review ingredient lists provided by the provider
- Be aware of personal or family allergens
- Ask the provider about allergens if uncertain
- Understand that cross-contamination may occur in home kitchens

## Storage Expectations

### Immediate Storage
- Refrigerate perishable food within 2 hours of pickup (1 hour in hot weather)
- Keep food at 4°C (40°F) or below in the refrigerator
- Freeze food if not consuming within safe timeframe
- Store food in clean, covered containers

### Consumption Timeframes
- Consume cooked leftovers within 3-4 days if refrigerated
- Consume frozen food within recommended timeframes
- Follow provider's specific recommendations when given
- When in doubt, throw it out

## Communication

Recipients should:

- Report any food safety concerns through the platform
- Provide feedback to providers about food quality
- Ask questions about handling if uncertain
- Communicate promptly if unable to pick up reserved food

## Limitations

KhaanaShare does not:

- Guarantee food safety after pickup
- Provide refrigeration or storage solutions
- Test food for safety or quality
- Provide medical advice regarding food consumption
- Assume liability for foodborne illness

Recipients understand that food rescue involves some uncertainty and exercise personal judgment when consuming rescued food.
